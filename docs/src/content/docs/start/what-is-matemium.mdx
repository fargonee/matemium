Matemium is an **agentic desktop studio for turning structured knowledge into
visual stories**. It combines project planning, AI-assisted authoring, a
layout-to-animation compiler, local rendering, and output management.

Mathematics is where Matemium began because equations, graphs, geometric objects,
and precise spatial relationships place unusually high demands on an animation
system. The same underlying model can be used for physics, chemistry, computing,
engineering, economics, biology, history, philosophy, language learning, and
other structured explanations.

## What makes it different?

Traditional animation tools begin with a timeline. Matemium begins with the
**structure of the idea**.

## What can it deliver?

- Portrait 9:16 and landscape 16:9 video
- Complete reasoning tapes as upright, uncropped PNG or PDF documents
- Portable `.matemium.zip` project archives containing editable source, briefs,
  imported media, renders, and output history
- Editable copies of eleven bundled cross-subject source examples

See [Import, export, and move projects](/desktop/import-export/) for the current
desktop controls.

## Who is it for?

- Educators and subject experts creating visual explanations
- Technical creators producing lessons, reels, and reference material
- Developers who want a declarative layer over Manim
- Contributors extending a source-available creative tool
- Anyone explaining an idea that benefits from structure, diagrams, transformation,
  and motion

Matemium is not intended to replace a general nonlinear video editor. It is
strongest when the content has an explainable structure: steps, relationships,
states, causes, comparisons, or spatial objects.

## What stays local?

The desktop application owns the workspace and launches the bundled rendering
engine. Rendering does not require uploading your project or generated media to
Matemium.

Optional AI assistance uses either:

- A provider account you connect, with OpenRouter as the default path; or
- A compatible local model managed on your computer.

Matemium does not sell model credits or provide a shared model quota.
