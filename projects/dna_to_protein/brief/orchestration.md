# Orchestration — DNA to Protein

- Landscape 16:9; preserve one `BiologyScaleWorld` identity from cell to
  schematic protein.
- Begin with a labelled cell boundary, then show labelled nucleus, chromosome,
  gene region, and DNA region as separate levels of detail.
- State explicitly that cell/nucleus/gene moves are cinematic levels of detail,
  not metrically literal scale travel.
- Generate aligned DNA, RNA, amino-acid, and stop rows from one structured
  teaching sequence.
- Open the selected DNA before assembling RNA through five visible progress
  states generated from the same sequence records.
- Show pre-mRNA, intron departure, exon joining, mature mRNA, and export as
  separate spatial states; keep translation in the cytoplasm.
- Follow mature RNA through a visible pore before revealing the ribosome.
- For every codon, highlight it, bring in a labelled tRNA/amino acid, grow the
  peptide, and advance the ribosome; stop has no amino acid or tRNA delivery.
- Show a labelled four-residue folded trace only after its limitation tape;
  never imply structure prediction.
- Use only cell and nucleus boundary spheres; remove them during molecular
  scenes. Close on `DNA → RNA → CODONS → PROTEIN`.
- Use large, high-contrast DejaVu Sans text and short progressive statements.
