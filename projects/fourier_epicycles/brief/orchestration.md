# Orchestration — Fourier Epicycles

## Format and visual system

- Landscape 16:9 master; dark navy background.
- White editorial typography with cyan, violet, coral, mint, amber, blue, pink,
  and teal harmonic identities.
- The square-wave target is always restrained gray; the active reconstruction
  is warm yellow.
- The scene is one vertical tape with large section separation. Automatic
  camera travel moves between conceptual stations.

## Beat choreography

### F00 — Hook

Reveal the editorial title and question, then create the reference/reconstruction
plot. Keep the frame quiet enough that the abrupt edge is the dominant shape.

### F01 — Projection

Show circle geometry and sine plot as a single flex composition. Alternate
diagram morph and marker morph through one cycle, producing an intentionally
stepped, inspectable motion rather than a decorative continuous spinner. End
with synchronized semantic highlighting before introducing notation.

### F02 — Spectrum

Create odd-frequency stems in stable harmonic colors. Pulse the first five
semantic series in order. Then reveal the compact summation formula. Do not
show a dense separate legend; color identity is already established by the
stems and later circles.

### F03 — Reconstruction

Keep the target and active sum registered under stable IDs. Morph the entire
generic plot through 1, 2, 3, 5, and 9-term data states. A compact `N = …`
counter changes beside it; the general series formula remains in the preceding
beat and is not expanded into an unreadable line.

### F04 — Synchronized views

Place nested epicycles left and the partial-sum plot right. For each phase,
morph both registered visuals. The current implementation sequences the paired
morphs rather than claiming shared-clock binding. Emphasize the final nested
tip and plot marker together to establish semantic correspondence.

### F05 — Gibbs close-up

Use a narrowed x-range around the jump. Morph from 3 to 7, 15, and 25 terms.
Apply a restrained overlay focus after the final state, then present the
two-part conclusion. Avoid a numerical overshoot claim in the video.

### F06 — Finale

Use three evenly sized typographic cards for frequency, amplitude, and phase.
Reveal the symbolic synthesis and product line. End on an isolate focus of the
title, preserving a clean hold for website use.

## Motion and pacing notes

- Diagram/plot phase steps: roughly 0.55–0.68 seconds each.
- Term-addition morphs: roughly 1.15 seconds each.
- Text is intentionally short because this production has no narration.
- Camera moves carry conceptual transitions; within each station, object
  identity is preserved by morphs and state transitions.
- A future reactive-clock engine feature could make paired phase motion truly
  simultaneous. This version does not advertise that unimplemented behavior.
