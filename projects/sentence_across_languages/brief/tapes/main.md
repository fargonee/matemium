# Visible content — Sentence across languages

## C00 — Meaning before language

- Actor: student.
- Action: read.
- Object: definite book.
- Time: today.

## C01 — Transform

- English: `The student is reading the book today.`
- Uzbek: `Talaba bugun kitobni o‘qiyapti.`
- Preserve semantic node identity while position and surface expression change.

## C02 — Direct comparison

- English neutral example: actor–action–object–time.
- Uzbek neutral example: actor–time–object–action.

## C03 — Morphology

- `kitob-ni`: book plus definite direct-object marker.
- `o‘qi-yap-ti`: read plus progressive plus third-person form.

## C04 — Reading path

- Cue the four Uzbek tokens sequentially without bundled audio.
- State that pronunciation recording requires separate review.

## C05 — Variation boundary

- Neutral patterns do not define every sentence in either language.
- A structural gloss is not a word-for-word natural translation.
