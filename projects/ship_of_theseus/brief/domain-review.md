## Claims checked

1. Qualitative identity/sameness is distinct from numerical identity.
2. Gradual replacement motivates a persistence question.
3. Reassembly of removed parts creates a rival claimant.
4. Replacement and reassembly cannot both be numerically identical to the one
   original if they are distinct from each other.
5. The thought experiment does not itself force one uncontested solution.
