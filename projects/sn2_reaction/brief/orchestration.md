- Portrait 9:16; begin directly in the persistent 3D reaction world.
- Identify C*, O, H, and Br before asking the viewer to decode movement.
- Use one establishing camera move, then lock the camera for alignment, bond
  change, transition state, energy replay, and stereochemical comparison.
- Establish the C—Br axis, `BACKSIDE`, `LG SIDE`, attack direction, and 180°
  relationship before moving the nucleophile.
- Drive molecule geometry, partial bonds, and energy marker from the same
  project-local `progress` state.
- Freeze at progress 0.5 with both partial bonds, transition-state notation,
  and a fixed reference plane visible.
- Remove the energy panel before the product comparison so each beat has one
  visual question.
- Compare before and after under the same camera and reference plane.
- Use the only tape for the final synthesis: backside attack, simultaneous bond
  change, and inversion are one concerted event.
- Treat the procedural geometry and energy curve as qualitative authored
  models, not computed chemistry.
