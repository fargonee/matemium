# Visible contexts — SN2 Reaction

1. **Identity world:** introduce the reaction and label C*, O, H, and Br.
2. **Alignment world:** lock the side view; show the C—Br axis, backside,
   leaving-group side, attack arrow, and 180° approach.
3. **Concerted world:** advance one reaction coordinate while the C—O bond
   forms, the C—Br bond breaks, and the substituent geometry inverts.
4. **Transition-state freeze:** show both dashed partial bonds, a reference
   plane, and transition-state notation at progress 0.5.
5. **Energy observation:** replay the same reaction progress with a labelled
   energy curve and synchronized marker in the same world.
6. **Inversion comparison:** show before and after together under one fixed
   camera and reference plane.
7. **Closing tape:** state the single takeaway in large mobile-safe type.

The tape never overlays the molecule. All chemistry before the finale remains
inside one persistent registered world.
