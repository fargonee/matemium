# Visible content — SN2 Reaction

## Beat C00 — One step, two bonds

- `ONE STEP. TWO BONDS.`
- “How can a new bond form at the same moment another bond breaks?”
- Generic substitution equation
- SN2 identified as a concerted passage.

Viewer outcome: the central puzzle is simultaneity, not a list of products.

## Beat C01 — Backside geometry

- Stable-ID molecular projection with nucleophile, stereocentre, leaving group,
  and three persistent substituent identities.
- The nucleophile approaches opposite the C—Br bond.
- Nucleophile and leaving bond receive semantic emphasis.

Viewer outcome: “backside” has a concrete spatial referent.

## Beat C02 — Transition state and inversion

- The molecular diagram morphs to a state containing two partial bonds.
- `TRANSITION STATE`
- Explicit statement that it is not an isolable intermediate.
- The diagram morphs to products with the three substituents inverted.

Viewer outcome: bond changes and inversion are one concerted event.

## Beat C03 — Energy coordinate

- One smooth reaction-coordinate curve with no intermediate well.
- Registered progress marker stages:
  - reactants;
  - energy maximum / transition state;
  - products.
- Bimolecular rate law: `rate = k[Nu⁻][R—Br]`.

Viewer outcome: the molecular and energetic descriptions share the same three
states.

## Beat C04 — Steric access

- Backside path marked accessible at a less crowded carbon.
- The same diagram morphs to a crowded center.
- The approach path becomes hindered.

Viewer outcome: steric crowding changes access to the required geometry.

## Beat C05 — Synthesis

- `CONCERTED`
- Backside approach + bond-making + bond-breaking + inversion
- “Four observations. One molecular event.”
- Signature transition-state motif.

Viewer outcome: four separate textbook facts collapse into one causal model.
