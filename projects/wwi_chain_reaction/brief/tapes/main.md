# Visible content — World War I chain reaction

## C00 — Central question

- Ask how a regional assassination became a European war.
- State that alliances constrained choices but did not behave as automatic
  dominoes.

## C01 — Thirty-eight days

- Present nine ordered events from 28 June through 4 August 1914.
- Color-code trigger, diplomatic decisions, mobilization, and war.

## C02 — Decision relationships

- Show the seven central state actors as a semantic network.
- State selected backing, ultimatum, support, planning, neutrality/entry, and
  alignment relationships without claiming completeness.

## C03 — Relative geography

- Locate the decision centers and Balkan crisis sites schematically.
- Explicitly disclose that this is not a 1914 border map.

## C04 — Contingency

- Ultimatum and reply.
- Proposed mediation.
- Mobilization orders.

## C05 — Causal layers

- Trigger.
- Structural pressures.
- Constraints.
- Decisions.
- Close by distinguishing chronological order from causal interpretation.
